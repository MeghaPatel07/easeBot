A beach wedding in May, specifically with a sundowner (sunset) vibe, offers a gorgeous color palette to play with. Since May can be quite warm, you’ll want the design to feel breezy, elegant, and refreshing.

Here are a few design directions for your wedding cards:

### 1. The "Golden Hour" Gradient
Since you mentioned a **Sundowner**, the sky is your best inspiration.
* **Color Palette:** A soft blend of dusty orange, warm gold, and deep violet—mimicking the sky as the sun hits the horizon.
* **Design Element:** Use a **watercolor wash** background. Instead of sharp lines, let the colors bleed into each other.
* **Typography:** Use a clean, thin sans-serif font for the details and a flowing, metallic gold foil script for your names.

### 2. Tropical Minimalism (The "May Breeze")
May is the heart of tropical season, so lean into lush textures but keep the layout "quiet."
* **Materials:** Use **Vellum (translucent) paper** overlays. It feels like sea mist.
* **Design Element:** A single, blind-embossed (no color, just texture) palm leaf or a spray of white orchids in the corner.
* **The Twist:** Use a "Sand" textured paper for the main card to give guests a tactile feeling of the beach.

### 3. Nautical Chic (Sundowner Sophistication)
For a more formal beach vibe that leans into the evening party aspect.
* **Color Palette:** Navy blue, champagne gold, and crisp white.
* **Design Element:** Incorporate a subtle **geometric pattern** that looks like waves or fish scales, but done in gold foil.
* **The Details:** Seal the envelope with a **custom wax seal** featuring a seashell or a sun icon.

### 4. The "Message in a Bottle" (Modern Version)
A playful take on a classic beach theme without being "cheesy."
* **Format:** A **tri-fold card** that unfolds vertically. The top panel shows the bright morning sun, the middle shows the sunset (sundowner), and the bottom shows a starry night.
* **Illustration:** A hand-drawn map of the coastline where the wedding is held, including little icons for the ceremony spot and the bar.
