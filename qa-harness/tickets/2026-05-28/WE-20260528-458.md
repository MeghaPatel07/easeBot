## Description

At 200% browser zoom on desktop (a common low-vision setting; required by WCAG 1.4.4 Resize Text), the AnalyticsConsent banner overlays the empty-state hero ("Hi! I'm here to help...") and obscures the "Help me decide" affordance plus the "Not sure where to start?" suggestion card. The Decline/Accept buttons themselves remain reachable but the page's primary information density collapses.

The site does NOT trigger horizontal scroll at 200% (correct — content reflows), but the consent overlay is positioned by fixed offsets that don't scale with zoom.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open `/` at 1280×800
2. Browser zoom 200% (Cmd-+ twice, or zoom CSS via DevTools)
3. Observe consent banner overlays hero

## Expected

At ≥150% zoom, banner converts to inline-non-overlay or top-of-page strip.

## Actual

Banner overlays hero; key onboarding CTAs partly hidden.
