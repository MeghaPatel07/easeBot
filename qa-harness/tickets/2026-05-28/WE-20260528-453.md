## Description

At iPhone SE 320×568, the AnalyticsConsent banner sits roughly mid-viewport and:
- on `/login`: completely covers the `Enter your email` input field. A new user cannot tap email-input until they dismiss the banner. Decline/Accept buttons are themselves small (66×28) on this width.
- on `/help`: completely covers the **Company Details card** (the only place vendor/support contact info shows). Below-fold FAQ still readable but the support card is obscured.

Distinct from -068 (chat chip rail) and -058 (mascot clipping). This affects two separate marketing pages with their own dominant content.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Clear cookies/localStorage
2. Open `/login` at 320×568 → email field hidden by banner
3. Open `/help` at 320×568 → Company Details card hidden by banner

## Expected

Banner does not cover form fields or info cards on any page; it should either pin to the absolute top or bottom in a non-modal way that doesn't overlay focusable content.

## Actual

Banner blocks form input on /login; blocks support contact on /help.
