## Description

logo.png ships as a 4874×3078 PNG at 297KB. The displayed dimensions are likely <500px wide. Lighthouse network-requests shows it as the largest image (296KB transferred) on the initial load. easebot.png is also 275×300 at 226KB which is much larger than necessary.

This image alone is 25% of the total page weight (1460KB on /chat).

## Steps to reproduce

1. file dist/images/logo.png
2. Open / and inspect <img src='.../logo.png'> rendered size vs natural size

## Expected

Logo served at ≤ 2× displayed size, WebP/AVIF format with PNG fallback, ≤ 30KB.

## Actual

Logo served at 4874×3078 PNG (297KB).

- Lighthouse JSON: `qa-harness/evidence/perf-2026-05-28/lh-*-prod.json`

## Notes

Fix: regenerate logo at 600×800 max with WebP. Same for easebot.png. Consider <picture> with srcset + AVIF.
