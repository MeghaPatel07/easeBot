## Description

Vibe descriptions use words like:
- "jaali" (perforated stone/wood screen)
- "jharokha" (overhanging enclosed balcony)
- "filigree", "damask", "brocade", "zari" (textile terms)
- "chiaroscuro" (art technique)
- "monstera", "heliconia" (botanical)
- "macrame", "pampas" (decorative)

For non-South-Asian users encountering "jaali", or non-art-history users encountering "chiaroscuro", these are opaque. WCAG 3.1.3 (AAA) wants a mechanism to surface definitions for jargon.

## Expected

- Provide a glossary tooltip on hover/focus for jargon words
- Use `<dfn title="A perforated stone screen common in Mughal architecture">jaali</dfn>` pattern
- Or: rewrite at grade-8 level (see -1057)

## Actual

Jargon without glossing.
