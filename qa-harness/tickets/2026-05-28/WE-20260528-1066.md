## Description

Tailwind's `animate-in fade-in slide-in-from-top-2 duration-300` and `animate-[shimmer_1.8s_ease-in-out_infinite]` (ChatMessages:596) are CSS animations. The global `prefers-reduced-motion` reset zeroes `animation-duration` and `transition-duration`. HOWEVER:

- `animate-[shimmer_…_infinite]` with `iteration-count: 1` (the reset clamps to 1) still plays one cycle → 1.8s of shimmer even under reduced-motion
- `transition-all duration-700` (line 585 blur-up image) at duration 0.01ms becomes a hard cut — actually acceptable
- Slide-in-from-top-2 with duration: 0.01ms = hard appear — fine
- `animate-pulse` (lines 589, 324) — single iteration is still a pulse → distracting

For users with vestibular disorders or severe motion sensitivity (which prefers-reduced-motion is designed to serve), even a single pulse of an infinite animation can trigger symptoms.

## Expected

Wrap individual `animate-*` classes in `motion-safe:` (Tailwind variant) so they don't apply at all when prefers-reduced-motion is set, rather than relying on the global duration clamp:
- `motion-safe:animate-pulse`
- `motion-safe:animate-[shimmer_1.8s_ease-in-out_infinite]`

## Actual

Single-iteration leakage through the global reset.

## Notes

P3 because most users won't notice, but it's a published Tailwind pattern and 5-minute fix. The shimmer in particular is the strongest offender.
