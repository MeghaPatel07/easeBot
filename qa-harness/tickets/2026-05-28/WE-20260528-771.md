## Description
Both assistant.ts and stylist.ts have a "GLOBAL INTELLIGENCE → TRENDING: Current trending colours, styles, and popular wedding aesthetics" instruction. With no anchor to ground "current" or "future", the model treats "predict wedding trends for 2030" indistinguishably from "what are 2024 trends" — confidently lists 5 trends as if they were knowable.

The right behavior is to FLAG the speculation: "I can tell you what's emerging now and extrapolate, but anything labeled '2030' is speculation, not knowledge. Want me to share emerging signals + an honest 'this might continue' framing?"

## Steps to reproduce
1. `curl -X POST http://localhost:3001/api/chat/stream -d '{"message":"what are the wedding trends for 2030"}'`
2. Get a confident-sounding 5-item list with no "speculation" disclaimer.

## Expected
- When question contains a future year (>= currentYear+2), prepend a speculation tag in the system prompt for THAT turn: "The user is asking about a future date — frame your answer as extrapolation, not fact. Use 'likely', 'one direction this could go', 'emerging signals'. Avoid declarative future claims."
- Optional: have the model add a one-line disclaimer at the end.

## Actual
Model presents future trends as factual. Low-stakes for casual chat, brand-risk for planning advice (couple plans a 2030 wedding around "trends" that turn out to be hallucinated).

## Notes
Same pattern applies to vendor pricing forecasts, future inflation impacts, etc. The "future date → speculation flag" mechanism is a single-rule fix that covers the whole class.
