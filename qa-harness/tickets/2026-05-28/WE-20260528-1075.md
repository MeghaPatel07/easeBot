## Description

WCAG 2.2 introduced 2.4.12 Focus Appearance (originally AA, demoted to AAA in WCAG 2.2 release). It requires the focus indicator be:
1. At least as large as a 2-CSS-pixel-thick outline around the perimeter
2. Have at least 3:1 contrast change from the unfocused state
3. Not be entirely obscured

Slider thumb (`slider.tsx:21`): `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-primary/50 focus-visible:ring-offset-2`. `ring-primary/50` is the brand primary at 50% opacity — depending on background this drops below 3:1.

Many other focus rings in the app use `ring-primary/20` to `/40` for visual "softness" — design choice that fails 2.4.12 contrast.

## Expected

Audit all `focus-visible:ring-*` Tailwind classes. Ensure:
- `ring-2` minimum
- Color at full opacity OR a high-contrast accent color
- `ring-offset-2` so the ring is visible against any background

## Actual

Soft rings, opacity-dimmed → fail 2.4.12 contrast target.

## Notes

Sprint 1+2 covered 2.4.7 (visible at all). This is the next-level audit: visible AND meeting size + contrast targets.
