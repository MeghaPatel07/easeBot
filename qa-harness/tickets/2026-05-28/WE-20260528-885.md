## Description

When recording is active the ChatInput renders icon-only buttons (StopCircle to send, X to cancel) with no visible text label. Audit of ChatInput.tsx shows aria-labels missing on these icon-only buttons. VoiceOver reads them as "button" with no context, blocking screen reader users from cancelling a stuck recording.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open /chat, tap mic to start recording.
2. Turn on VoiceOver (Cmd+F5 on Mac).
3. Tab to the recording-control buttons.
4. Observe screen-reader announcement: just "button".

## Expected

`aria-label="Stop recording and send"` and `aria-label="Cancel recording"`.

## Actual

No labels.

## Evidence

- ChatInput.tsx recording-state buttons (audit).

Sprint 1 covered voice-mode visual states; this is the a11y gap.
