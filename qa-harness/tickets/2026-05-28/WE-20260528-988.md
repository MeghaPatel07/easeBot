1. Sign in with Remember-me checked, email saved.
2. Sign out. Tab A and Tab B both show SignInModal with email pre-filled.
3. Tab A, uncheck Remember, sign in again, sign out.
4. Tab B, observe SignInModal.

## Expected
Tab B no longer pre-fills email.

## Actual
- Tab B's mount-time read happened before Tab A wrote; no storage event, still pre-filled.
