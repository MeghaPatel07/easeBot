## Description

At 412 and 360 widths, the "Compare Plans in Detail" table shrinks all columns to fit container — resulting in row text appearing at ~6–8px (well below 12px legibility minimum). Rows like "PDF support" / "CSV export" / "Concierge support" all overlap to near-invisibility. Free/Pro/Pro Max column headers are also tiny.

Distinct from -057 (3-tier vs 4-tier regression). This is about table rendering at narrow widths NOT switching to a stacked / per-plan card-comparison format.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open `/pricing` at 412 or 360
2. Scroll past tier cards to "Compare Plans in Detail"
3. Note text is unreadable at native size

## Expected

Below ~600px, the comparison table should either: (a) switch to per-plan stacked feature lists, (b) horizontally scroll a fixed minimum-width table with overflow indicator, or (c) hide non-essential columns.

## Actual

Table squashes to fit; all text shrinks below readable threshold.
