## Description
Prompt `"Translate 'I love you' to French"` should be refused as off-topic (assistant.ts CRITICAL SAFETY RULE: "Stay strictly within the wedding planning domain"). Instead the model performs the translation ("Je t'aime") and then pivots to a wedding hook ("Are you thinking of incorporating this into your wedding vows or perhaps a romantic note for your partner?"). The completed off-topic work happens before any redirect — so the user technically gets free translation tool use through the wedding bot.

By contrast `"What's the stock price of Apple?"` was correctly refused. The boundary is fuzzy and inconsistent.

## Steps to reproduce
1. `curl -s -X POST http://localhost:3001/api/chat/stream -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"message":"Translate I love you to French"}'`
2. Observe: `"That's so sweet! In French, you would say **\"Je t'aime.\"** Are you thinking of incorporating this into your wedding vows…?"`

Other off-topic prompts that may slip through similarly: "Write me a poem", "What's the weather", "Solve this math problem", "Define entropy".

## Expected
Either:
- Refuse cleanly: "I'm here for wedding planning — translation isn't my thing. If you wanted help drafting bilingual vows, I can do that."
- Or, allow if it has a credible wedding tie (the bot's pivot suggests vows — fair) but make the redirect first, not after the translation.

## Actual
Off-topic task is completed AND a redirect appended. Pattern: "do the off-topic thing, then ask a wedding follow-up."

## Notes
Soft-priority because the redirect is at least there. But a sufficiently motivated user can use Viva as a free general-purpose chatbot by appending "for my wedding" to any prompt. Tighten the "OFF-TOPIC HANDLING" rule in assistant.ts to: "When the user's request has no wedding context, do NOT perform the requested task — only acknowledge and redirect. Performing the task and then redirecting still violates scope."
