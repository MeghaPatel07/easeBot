## Description

Voice recording auto-stops at 60 seconds (per useVoice.ts:131 comment "60-second auto-stop timeout"). There is no warning to the user that they're approaching the limit:

- No countdown indicator in the UI as the timer crosses 50s / 55s
- No SR announcement
- No way to extend the limit
- The recording just ends — and is sent — which is a destructive default

WCAG 2.2.1 requires that users can extend a timeout, OR that the timeout be ≥ 20 hours, OR essential exception. Voice STT is arguably essential timing but the user should at least be warned and offered cancel.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Tap mic
2. Speak for 55+ seconds
3. Observe — recording cuts off at 60s with no warning

## Expected

At 50s remaining-10s, the timer color shifts to warning + announce "10 seconds remaining for voice recording". At expiry, announce "Voice recording stopped at 60 second limit" rather than silent submit.

## Actual

Silent cut-off and auto-submit.

## Notes

This intersects with cognitive-a11y. Users with motor / processing limitations may need extra time. Consider raising the limit OR adding a "continue recording" affordance at 60s.
