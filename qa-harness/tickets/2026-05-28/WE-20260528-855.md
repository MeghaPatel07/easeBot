## Description

Empty-state UX is inconsistent and dead-end across the four list-views. None offer a CTA button. Audit:

| View | Icon | Heading | Copy | CTA Button |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gallery | Sparkles in pink-box | (no heading) | "No images yet. Ask TheWeddingBot to generate some visuals." | **none** |
| Notifications | Bell in gray-box | "No notifications" | "You'll see reminders for upcoming deadlines here" | **none** |
| Timeline (no wedding date) | Calendar in gray-box | "No timeline items yet" | "Add due dates to your checklist items or create calendar events…" | **none** |
| Budget (no categories) | (none, footer message) | (none) | "Add categories to start tracking" (footer only, easy to miss) | **none** |

A new user who lands on /budget with no plan has a blank screen + tiny footer caption, no obvious next action. Same on /timeline — copy *describes* what to do but doesn't *link* to checklists or event-creation UI. On /notifications a user can do nothing here at all — empty-state copy is informative only.

Inconsistency: Gallery uses pink primary-tinted icon; others use gray; Budget has no centered hero at all.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Sign up with a fresh account.
2. Open the sidebar; click Gallery, then Notifications, then Timeline, then Budget.
3. Observe four different empty-state visual treatments, none with a button.

## Expected

Standard pattern:
1. Centered icon (consistent style: rounded-xl, primary/10 bg, primary/70 icon).
2. h3 heading.
3. Short helpful copy.
4. **Primary CTA button** that does the next action:
   - Gallery: "Generate your first image" → opens VibeComposer with intro tooltip.
   - Notifications: "Set up reminders" → links to Settings → Notifications (or to /timeline).
   - Timeline: "Add your first event" → opens TimelineEventDialog.
   - Budget: "Add a category" → opens AddCategoryDialog inline.

## Actual

Decorative copy only; users have to discover the path forward themselves.

## Evidence

- GalleryView.tsx:117-128 (no `<Button>` element).
- NotificationPanel.tsx:170-184 (no `<Button>`).
- TimelineView.tsx:715-734 (toolbar above but no CTA in the hero).
- BudgetDashboard.tsx:400-411 (empty-state is the footer caption — not a hero at all).

Compare with /chat empty-state which DOES have an obvious composer + mode-selector + suggestion chips. Sprint 1 -052 noted "mobile empty-state mascot overlaps text" on the chat-empty hero specifically — that's a different empty state.
