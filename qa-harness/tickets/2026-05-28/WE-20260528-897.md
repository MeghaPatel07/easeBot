## Description

BudgetDashboard footer shows percentage-used and amount-remaining chips. When `budget.categories.length === 0`, these compute to `0% used` and `₹0 left` — but those numbers are misleading. The user sees "0% used / 0 left" and may think they've spent nothing of nothing (true but confusing). Better: hide the chips entirely when there's no budget, OR render `—%` and `— left`.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Fresh signup → /budget.
2. Observe footer: "0% used" and "0 left" (or currency-formatted zero).

## Expected

When no categories, render `—%` and `—` placeholders (or hide chips).

## Actual

`0%` / `0` literal.

- BudgetDashboard.tsx:407-410.
