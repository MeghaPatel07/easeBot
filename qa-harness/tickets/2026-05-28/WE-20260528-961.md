## Steps to reproduce
1. Open Settings, Personalization. Change wedding date from 2026-12-15 to 2027-03-20. Save.
2. Without reload: open Timeline view (sidebar). Observe `WeddingMarker` date label.
3. Open Progress view. Observe "Days until wedding" hero number.
4. Open Index `/`, observe the daysUntil banner (if any).
5. Open Planner, observe "urgency" indicators on checklist items.

## Expected
All four surfaces update within 1s.

## Actual
- PersonalizationTab calls `useAccount.updateProfile` which invalidates `['account','me']` query. But weddingDate is read from `useAuth().profile.weddingDate` directly in Index.tsx:1341, 1345 — not from useAccount.
- TimelineView gets `weddingDate={profile?.weddingDate?.toDate?.() ?? null}` as a prop drilled from Index. Since `AuthContext.profile` is loaded once via `getDoc` and never refreshed (no listener), `profile.weddingDate` stays stale across all surfaces.
- All 4 surfaces show OLD date until full reload.

## Root cause
Same root cause as 151/152: `AuthContext.profile` is frozen at sign-in; the entire countdown surface family depends on it.
