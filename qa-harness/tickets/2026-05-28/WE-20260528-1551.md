## Description
When `mode` is omitted (defaults to non-planner) or set to `styler`/`knowledge`, the AI fabricates the current date as ~2 years in the past. Tested 2026-05-28: model said "If today is October 7, 2023, your wedding date will fall on April 7, 2024" for a "6 months from today" query. The model invented a date in the past because the non-planner prompts (`stylist.ts`, `knowledge.ts`) do NOT inject `today's date` at all. Only `planner.ts` injects `const today = new Date().toISOString().split('T')[0]`.

## Steps to reproduce
1. `curl -X POST localhost:3001/api/chat/stream -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"message":"We are getting married in exactly 6 months from today. What will the wedding date be?"}'`
2. Observe response: "If today is October 7, 2023... April 7, 2024, which is a Sunday."

## Expected
- "Today is 2026-05-28; 6 months out lands on ~2026-11-28 (Saturday)."

## Actual
- Model fabricates today as Oct 7 2023 and gives April 7 2024.

## Fix sketch
Inject `Today's date is ${new Date().toISOString().split('T')[0]}.` into all active mode prompts (`stylist.ts`, `knowledge.ts`), or hoist to a shared base-system block in the controller. Add unit eval: "what is today?" must contain current year.
