## Description

The brand-logo `<a>` link in ChatHeader.tsx:218 has `className="sm:hidden …"` — meaning the logo is shown ONLY on mobile (<640px) and hidden on tablet/desktop. The reasoning is likely "the sidebar at >=sm shows the logo, so we don't need it in the header too" — but:
- The sidebar can be collapsed/hidden — then desktop chat has NO brand mark anywhere.
- On tablets where the sidebar overlays as a drawer (>=md but <lg per ChatSidebar logic) the logo isn't visible until user opens the drawer.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open /chat at 1280×800.
2. Collapse the sidebar (Cmd+B).
3. Observe: no logo anywhere on the page. Header is buttons-only.

## Expected

Either:
- Show logo on all breakpoints (small "WeddingEase" mark top-left).
- Or, when sidebar is collapsed, conditionally show logo in header.

## Actual

Logo hidden at sm+; collapsed sidebar = no brand anywhere.

## Evidence

- ChatHeader.tsx:218 — `className="sm:hidden …"` on the logo `<a>`.

Sprint 1 -051 was about logo wrap on mobile; this is the inverse — logo absent on desktop.
