## Description
At `history.length > 10`, controller summarizes `olderMessages = history.slice(0, history.length - 5)` into a single assistant message. With a 51-turn history where turn 1 stated "Hi I am Krish marrying Priya December 2026 Bangalore 200 guests $25k budget", the model — after summarization — reports it does NOT have any of that info: "I don't have any of that information from our conversation so far." All five anchors lost.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Build 51-entry history where turn 1 contains anchor facts and turns 2-50 are unrelated chatter.
2. Send "After all those tips, can you recall: my name, partner name, wedding month, city, guest count, budget?"
3. Model replies it has no such info.

## Expected
The summarizer's `systemPrompt` already says "Preserve all proper nouns and numbers exactly" — but this is not happening. Either the summary is being truncated, the anchor turn is being squeezed out, or the summarizer is being given turns that no longer contain the anchor due to slice math at length>20.

## Actual
All anchor facts disappear from summary; model behaves as if conversation just started.

## Fix sketch
1. Inspect actual summary output for a 50-turn thread (log it).
2. Tighten summarizer system prompt: "MUST preserve verbatim: user name, partner name, dates, locations, budget, guest count."
3. Consider entity-extraction layer: parse anchor facts on every turn, keep them in a separate slot that bypasses the summarizer.
