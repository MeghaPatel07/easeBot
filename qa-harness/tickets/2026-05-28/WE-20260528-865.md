## Description

The "About you" and "Response style" `<textarea>` fields cap at 1500 chars (CUSTOM_INSTRUCTIONS_MAX). The textareas are rendered with a fixed `rows={4}` (~96px CSS height). Pasting a 1000-char paragraph fills the visible area; further text scrolls *inside* the textarea but:
- There's no visible scrollbar styling — the user doesn't realize there's content above/below the visible window.
- The counter ("1234 / 1500") below the field tells the user there's more but doesn't explain "scroll the field to see all of it."
- On mobile (textarea cramped further by Settings modal padding), 4 rows shows ~3 lines of text → 1500 chars means scrolling through ~15 invisible lines.

Better pattern: auto-grow the textarea up to the modal's max-h, then scroll inside the *modal* (which has a scrollbar) rather than inside the textarea (which doesn't).

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open Settings → Personalization (if visible — currently the tab is commented out per SettingsShell.tsx:81; pending re-enable).
2. Paste 1500-char Lorem into "About you".
3. Observe: only ~4 lines visible; scroll inside the textarea works but there's no visual cue.

## Expected

- Auto-grow textarea up to `max-h-[40vh]` (or similar), then internal scroll with a visible track.
- Counter copy: "1234 / 1500 — scroll to see all" when over the visible-window threshold.

## Actual

Hidden scrollable textarea; users may not realize they have extra content.
