## Description

Sample descriptions Flesch-Kincaid checked (approximated):

- "Pastel Mughal" — "ornate Mughal-inspired wedding: ivory and blush palette with antique gold filigree, damask textiles, carved marble jaalis and pointed Mughal arches…" — FK grade **~16 (college)**. Vocabulary includes "jaalis", "filigree", "damask", "chiaroscuro" elsewhere.
- "Royal Rajputana" — "dramatic chiaroscuro lighting and opulent editorial framing" — FK grade **~17**.
- "Minimalist Modern" — "strict architectural geometry, generous negative space, restrained monochrome palette, matte concrete surfaces, sparse sculptural floral installations" — FK grade **~14**.

Target per accessibility-cognitive guidance (and Hemingway/Plain-Language norms): **grade 8 or simpler** for primary product copy. Users with cognitive impairments, ESL users, or just users browsing on mobile cannot parse these.

## Expected

Rewrite vibe descriptions to grade-8 max. Example: "Pastel Mughal — Soft ivory and gold tones with arched windows, light flowers, and warm window light. A calm, ornate style."

## Actual

Multiple presets read like a fine-art catalogue.
