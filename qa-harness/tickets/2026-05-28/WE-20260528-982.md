## Steps to reproduce
1. Open PlannerView at 11:55 PM with 1 item due today.
2. Do not navigate or interact.
3. At 12:05 AM, check the overdue count.

## Expected
Item that was due "today" now shows overdue (since `today` is now tomorrow).

## Actual
- `today` captured at render time via `new Date()` in a memo with empty deps. No `setInterval(..., 60_000)` to recompute. Count stays at 0.
- User opens calendar morning of next day, sees a stale "no overdue" state.
