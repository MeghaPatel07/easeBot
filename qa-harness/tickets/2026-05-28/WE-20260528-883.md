## Description

The toast variants (line 26) include `data-[state=open]:slide-in-from-top-full data-[state=open]:sm:slide-in-from-bottom-full`. Mobile toasts slide DOWN from top; sm+ toasts slide UP from bottom. This is fine in isolation but:
- A user on mobile (toast slides from top) opens a /share/:token link on their MacBook (toast slides from bottom) — different motion idiom for the same notification.
- On tablets (>=sm, ~768px) toast slides from bottom; user is more likely to scroll content rather than focus on incoming notifications → the bottom-right corner may be off-screen on landscape iPad.

Tradeoff is small but the inconsistency is notable when documented.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open /chat on mobile, trigger toast (e.g. failed send).
2. Open same on desktop, trigger same.
3. Observe motion difference.

## Expected

Either:
- Always slide-from-top (consistent across breakpoints).
- Or use slide-in-from-right at sm+ (matches the bottom-right viewport position more naturally).

## Actual

Inconsistent motion direction across breakpoints.

- toast.tsx:26 cited.
