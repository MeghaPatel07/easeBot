## Description

Axe `region` rule reports content outside landmarks on every page listed above. Deep-scan confirms `main` count = 0 on Pricing (only `<header>`), Help (only `<header>`), Login (only `<header>`), NotFound (no landmarks at all besides the implicit `<body>`). Screen-reader rotor "Main Content" / "Skip to content" jump targets are unavailable; users must tab through every header link to reach page content.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open `/pricing` with VoiceOver.
2. Use Rotor → Landmarks.

## Expected

## Actual

## Evidence
