## Description

Inline link in surrounding text uses only color (`hover:underline` — underline appears only on hover) to distinguish itself from body copy. Users with low color perception cannot identify it as a link without hovering. Axe `link-in-text-block` violation captured.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open `/pricing`, scroll to the disclaimer/FAQ region near line 576.
2. Observe the in-paragraph link.

## Expected

Persistent underline (default browser behavior) OR a non-color secondary cue (icon, weight contrast meeting 3:1 with surrounding text).

## Actual

Link rendered as colored text inside the paragraph with no underline. Insufficient contrast with surrounding text body (Axe flagged).
