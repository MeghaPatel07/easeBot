## Description

At 400% browser zoom (WCAG 1.4.10 explicitly requires reflow up to 400%):
- `/login`: email input field displays as "Enter your ema" (truncated); the analytics consent banner shows "De" instead of "Decline"; the "Continue With Email" CTA spans two lines awkwardly; the right-side "WHAT YOU GET" panel is mostly off-canvas
- `/pricing`: tier cards overlap each other (Free, Pro, Pro Max overlap on the right edge); "Compare Plans" header is sliced into "Grow With Your Wedding" + half-card

WCAG 1.4.10 Reflow requires content to be presentable without horizontal scroll up to 400% zoom on a 1280×1024 viewport (equivalent to 320 CSS px). The login form does not reflow at this scale.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open `/login` at 1280×800
2. Zoom to 400% (Cmd-+ x 5)
3. Observe form fields and CTAs truncated; horizontal layout broken

## Expected

Layout reflows to single-column at 400% zoom; all fields, buttons, and CTAs remain fully readable and operable.

## Actual

Form clips off-canvas horizontally; primary CTAs unusable for low-vision users.

This fails **WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.10 (Level AA)** — Reflow. A wedding-platform serving older user base (parents of bride/groom) is likely to have a meaningful fraction of low-vision users at 200–400% zoom.
