## Description

At iPad Pro 1024 portrait (a common tablet for wedding planners per project notes), `/help`, `/privacy`, and `/terms` clamp content to ~560px (`max-w-xl`-ish) in the horizontal center, with ~230px of empty rosy gradient on each side. The pink-mosaic decorative tile is the most visible thing on screen, which makes the page look broken/half-loaded.

Distinct from -062 (ultrawide pricing scale-up). At iPad Pro portrait there is intentional content but the layout pretends the screen is smaller than it is.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open `/help`, `/privacy`, or `/terms` at 1024×1366 portrait
2. Note clamped content + large empty bands

## Expected

Marketing pages expand to a comfortable reading width (~720–820px) on 1024 portrait. Optional: use two-column layout for /privacy and /terms with ToC on the left.

## Actual

Content stays at ~560px wide; ~45% of viewport is decorative wash.
