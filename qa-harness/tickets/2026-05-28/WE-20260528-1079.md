## Description

Critical irony: the Help page itself has a "Something went wrong" failure toast. When submitting a support request fails:
- User loses what they typed (no draft persistence)
- Toast is the ONLY signal
- No alternate contact email surfaced ("Email us at support@weddingease.ai")
- No retry queue / offline support

For users who came to Help because they're already frustrated, an unexplained Help submission failure = full abandonment.

## Expected

- Persist support-form drafts to localStorage on input change
- On submit failure: show explicit fallback ("Please email support@weddingease.ai — your message is saved here for copy")
- "Copy my message" button to clipboard
- Direct mailto: link as last resort

## Actual

Toast "Something went wrong" + form possibly cleared.

## Notes

The MOST important error-recovery surface in the app, by definition (it's the help page).
