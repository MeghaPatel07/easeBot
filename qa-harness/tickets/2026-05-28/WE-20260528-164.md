## Description

`reminderService` exposes both `getDocs(query(remindersCol, orderBy('eventAt','desc')))` (one-shot) and `onSnapshot` (live). The Index.tsx wiring passes `onRefresh={refetchReminders}` to TimelineView — meaning the calendar view relies on imperative refetch instead of a live listener. When the assistant creates a reminder via a tool call, the Index page never knows to trigger `refetchReminders`. The reminder is invisible until the user manually refreshes OR navigates away and back.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open chat. Ask the assistant: "Remind me to send save-the-dates on June 1 at 3pm."
2. Once the assistant confirms ("OK, I've set a reminder for…"), navigate to Calendar / Timeline view without reloading.

## Expected

The new reminder appears within ~1 s of the assistant's confirmation.

## Actual

Reminder is missing from the calendar. Reload (or leaving and re-entering the view) makes it appear.

## Evidence

- Screenshot(s): `qa-harness/evidence/WE-20260528-164/screenshots/`
- Console log: `qa-harness/evidence/WE-20260528-164/console.log`

## Notes

Wire `subscribeToReminders` (already exported) into the Index/TimelineView mount instead of the imperative `refetchReminders` pattern.
