## Description

At ultrawide 1920+ the AnalyticsConsent banner is positioned at the viewport bottom-center, clamped to a narrow ~600px column. The main content (where it exists, e.g. /pricing comparison table at 974px wide) is wider than the banner, so the banner appears as a tiny floating tooltip in dead space rather than a page-level consent.

Visually disconnected — looks like a popup notification rather than a privacy banner.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open `/` or `/pricing` at 1920×1080
2. Observe consent banner is narrower than content above it; floats alone in bottom of viewport

## Expected

Banner adapts: at ≥1280, span full-width-of-content (or 80% viewport, whichever smaller) so it reads as a banner, not a popup. Alternative: convert to a top-strip on desktop.

## Actual

Banner stays clamped; visual relationship to page unclear.
