## Description

While the assistant streams, a typing-indicator (animated dots) appears. For SR users this is visually-only — they hear nothing for 3-20 seconds while waiting for the streamed response to settle into the live region. Conventional pattern: `<span role="status" aria-live="polite" aria-label="Assistant is typing">…</span>`. Without it, SR users may assume the app is frozen.

## Expected

Add `role="status" aria-live="polite"` to the typing-indicator container with `aria-label="Assistant is preparing a response"`. Announce ONCE on stream start; clear on stream end.

## Actual

Silent dots.

Pairs with -651 and -1067 (streaming announcement strategy).
