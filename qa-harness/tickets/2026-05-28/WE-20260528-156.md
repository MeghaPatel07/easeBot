## Description

`PersonalizationTab` writes wedding date via `useAccount.updateProfile({weddingDate: …})`. That correctly invalidates the `['account','me']` query, so `useAccount` consumers re-render — but `TimelineView` and `ProgressDashboard` do NOT consume `useAccount`. They are rendered in `Index.tsx` with `weddingDate={profile?.weddingDate ? (profile.weddingDate as any).toDate?.() ?? null : null}` where `profile` comes from `useAuth()` (see `Index.tsx:1341, 1345`). AuthContext profile is the frozen sign-in object (WE-20260528-152) → `weddingDate` prop stays at the OLD value → countdown ("X days until your wedding"), WeddingMarker placement on the timeline grid, and any progress-percent calculation based on remaining days all remain stale.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Sign in. Open Timeline view — note countdown ("63 days until your wedding").
2. Settings → Personalization → change wedding date by +30 days → Save (succeeds).
3. Switch back to Timeline view without reloading.

## Expected

Countdown updates to "33 days until your wedding" (and WeddingMarker moves on the grid) within ~1 s.

## Actual

Countdown still says 63. ProgressDashboard "days remaining" / time-elapsed bar unchanged. Reload required.

## Evidence

- Screenshot(s): `qa-harness/evidence/WE-20260528-156/screenshots/`
- Console log: `qa-harness/evidence/WE-20260528-156/console.log`

## Notes

Either pipe `weddingDate` from `useAccount()` (which is fresh after mutation) instead of `useAuth().profile`, OR install the user-doc onSnapshot listener (WE-20260528-152) so the AuthContext profile is always live.
