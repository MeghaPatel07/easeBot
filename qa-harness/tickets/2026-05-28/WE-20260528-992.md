## Steps to reproduce
1. Open app with all sidebars loaded (planner + saved items + reminders + threads).
2. Go offline for 90s.
3. Reconnect.

## Expected
Smooth re-sync, brief loading indicator.

## Actual
- All 4 listeners fire setState in the same tick, massive re-render. On low-end devices, visible jank / scroll jump.
- No debouncing / coalescing.
