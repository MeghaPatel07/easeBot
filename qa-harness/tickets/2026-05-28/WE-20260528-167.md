## Description

ChatSidebar reads `profile?.name` for both the initials and the visible label of the profile chip at the bottom of the sidebar. `profile` is the frozen AuthContext object. After updating name in Settings (via either SettingsModal or AccountTab + useAccount), the ChatSidebar chip is the most visible "ghost name" surface in the app — it sits in the user's eye-line constantly.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Sign in. Note the name in the bottom-left ChatSidebar chip.
2. Settings → Account → Edit name → Save (succeeds, toast visible).
3. Without reloading, look at the bottom-left chip.

## Expected

Chip updates within ~1 s.

## Actual

Chip remains at the previous name indefinitely.
