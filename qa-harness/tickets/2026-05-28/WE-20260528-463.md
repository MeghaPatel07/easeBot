## Description

At iPhone SE 320, the top of the /pricing page shows **the three tier cards (Free / Pro / Pro Max) rendered with the same vertical offset** — they stack but with insufficient gap, causing the Free header to overlap behind the Pro card title. The "Plans That Grow With Your Wedding" hero h1 is also clipped on the left edge by negative left margin.

Distinct from -057 (3-tier vs 4-tier regression). This is about layout integrity at 320 — the grid that works at ≥375 collapses at 320.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open `/pricing` at 320×568
2. Observe overlapping tier headers near top of page; observe hero h1 with left clip

## Expected

At 320, tier cards stack vertically with sufficient `gap-y` (≥16px) and the hero h1 has `padding-inline` that keeps text inside the viewport.

## Actual

Cards overlap; hero text clipped.

## Evidence

- /Users/krish/Desktop/easebot/qa-screenshots/2026-05-28-sprint2/iphone-se-320/pricing.png

## Notes

The Tailwind grid likely uses `lg:grid-cols-3 md:grid-cols-2` but no `gap-y-6` at base. Adding `gap-y-6` on the mobile base + `px-4` on the hero container fixes it.
