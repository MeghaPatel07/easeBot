## Steps to reproduce
1. As guest (no sign-in), open Tab A. Send 5 messages (close to cap of ~10).
2. Without closing Tab A, open Tab B in same browser context.
3. Tab B reads `easebot-guest-msg-count` from localStorage at mount, 5.
4. Tab B sends 5 more.
5. Tab A still thinks count is 5. Tab A user can send another 5.

## Expected
Quota is enforced per-browser, not per-tab. After cap is hit anywhere, all tabs block.

## Actual
- Guest count is per-tab in-memory after mount, written to localStorage but never re-read. No storage event handler.
- Quota bypassable by opening N tabs. P0 because: business impact, guest cap is the conversion driver to sign-up.
