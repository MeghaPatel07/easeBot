## Description

`grid-cols-3 sm:grid-cols-8` jumps from 3 columns (mobile) directly to 8 columns at sm (640). At iPad portrait 768 width, 8 cols = `(768 - 32 padding - 7*8 gap) / 8 = 86 px per tile`. With the action buttons overlay (44×44 touch target per Sprint 1 audit) the action buttons cover 51% of the tile width.

Better progression: `grid-cols-3 sm:grid-cols-4 md:grid-cols-6 lg:grid-cols-8`. This gives 4-col on phones-wide (640-767), 6-col on tablets (768-1023), 8-col on desktop.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open /?view=gallery on iPad portrait 768.
2. Observe cramped 8-column layout.
3. Hover/tap a tile — the action buttons take up most of the tile area.

## Expected

Stepwise progression as above.

## Actual

Density jump from 3 → 8 at the 640 breakpoint.

- GalleryView.tsx:144 cited.
