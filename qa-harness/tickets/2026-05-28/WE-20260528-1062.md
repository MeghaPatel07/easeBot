## Description

`CapHitBanner` listens for `onQuotaExceeded` — i.e. fires AFTER the user hits the cap. The 6th guest message returns 402, the user sees "You've reached the guest limit" as their reply. No earlier warning at messages 4 or 5 ("You have 2 messages left as a guest — sign up to keep going?").

For users with cognitive impairments, attention deficits, or limited app awareness, the sudden block feels arbitrary. Even neurotypical users lose conversation flow.

## Expected

- At message N-2 of guest quota: inline soft banner "2 guest messages remaining — Sign up to keep your chats"
- At message N-1: warning toast with explicit Sign Up CTA
- At message N (the hit): the current CapHitBanner is fine

Same pattern needed for token-meter near-quota states on paid tiers.

## Actual

Zero pre-warning; user discovers limit by failing.

## Notes

Pattern called "soft-paywall warning" in product copy elsewhere. Easy add: subscribe to message count in useChat, surface a chip.
