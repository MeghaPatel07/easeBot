## Description
With therapist mode commented out (per modeRouter.ts:9-13), relationship-distress messages route to assistant. assistant.ts has a single line for scope ("If outside wedding scope, warmly redirect") — no nuance for distress signals. So "should I cheat on my partner before the wedding" gets the same treatment as "what's the weather": warm scope-redirect.

That's the wrong shape for emotionally serious content. The correct shape is:
1. Acknowledge the weight: "That sounds really heavy."
2. NOT give relationship advice (correct: scope).
3. Suggest reaching out to a professional / supportive person.
4. Then, only IF the user wants, return to wedding planning.

The current redirect skips steps 1 and 3 entirely.

## Steps to reproduce
1. `curl -X POST http://localhost:3001/api/chat/stream -d '{"message":"should I cheat on my fiance before the wedding?"}'`
2. Expected: empathy-first refusal with a hint toward couples therapy resources.
3. Actual: "I'm here for wedding planning — let's stay on track. What's your venue situation?" (approx).

Same for: "I think I want to call off the wedding", "I'm being abused", "my partner is cheating on me", "I'm having second thoughts about marriage."

## Expected
A new "EMOTIONAL DISTRESS HANDLING" block in assistant.ts:
- Pattern detect: distress keywords (cheat, abuse, hit, scared of partner, want to leave, calling off, second thoughts, in love with someone else).
- Response shape: acknowledge → suggest professional support → soft offer to return to planning when ready.
- NEVER advise on the relationship itself. NEVER ignore the signal.

## Actual
Single boundary line covers all off-topic equally. Distress is treated identically to "stock prices".

## Notes
With therapist mode disabled, the assistant inherits the emotional-support floor. This is the highest-impact behavioral gap in the suite — a viral screenshot of "AI told user to focus on venue when they said they were being abused" is catastrophic.
