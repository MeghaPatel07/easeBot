## Steps to reproduce
1. Sign in as a Free user. Open the app.
2. Complete a PayU upgrade flow (backend writes tier mirror to Firestore + sets up subscription).
3. Webhook fires, server updates `usage` + `plan.tier`.
4. Look at the token meter in the header.

## Expected
Within ~1 s the meter shows the new tier (Pro, Studio, etc.) and the upgrade CTA disappears.

## Actual
- `useUsageStats.ts:5-23` queries `getAccountUsage` every `60_000` ms (`refetchInterval`), with `staleTime: 30_000`.
- The webhook ack does NOT push to the client. The client only knows on next refetch.
- Worst case: 60 s of stale Free tier label + still-visible upgrade CTAs (already paid for Pro).
- Refresh button in the meter does call `query.refetch()` manually, but most users won't know to click.

## Impact
P1 — paid users see "Free" badge for up to a minute after paying. They will (a) double-pay or (b) email support.

## Fix direction
1. After PayU success redirect, force `qc.invalidateQueries({ queryKey: ['account', 'usage'] })` and `['account', 'me']`.
2. Subscribe via Firestore `onSnapshot` to the user doc's `plan` / `usage` fields instead of polling.
3. Server pushes via SSE on tier change (heavier).

## Evidence
- `src/hooks/useUsageStats.ts:5-23`
- `src/components/pricing/UpgradeFlow.tsx:67` does have `useQueryClient()` — verify it invalidates on success.
