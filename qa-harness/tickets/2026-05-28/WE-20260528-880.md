## Description

On mobile, SettingsShell renders one tab at a time. User opens Plan & Billing → scrolls down past the tier cards to the invoices list → taps "Back" to return to the nav list → re-enters Plan & Billing. Expected: returns to where they scrolled. Actual: tab re-mounts and scrolls to top.

This is a UX paper cut on a high-content tab. Easy fix: each tab's content container persists its `scrollTop` in a ref when unmounting, restores on remount.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Resize to 375.
2. Open /?settings=plan-billing → scroll to bottom.
3. Tap Back.
4. Tap Plan & Billing again.
5. Observe content scrolled to top.

## Expected

Scroll position restored.

## Actual

Top.

## Evidence

- SettingsShell behavior verified by reading the mobile-back component logic.

## Notes

Low priority — most users don't scroll deep in Settings tabs except Plan & Billing.
