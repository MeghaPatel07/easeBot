## Steps to reproduce
1. Tab A: play an audio response, set speed to 1.5×.
2. Tab B: play any audio response. Speed reverts to 1.0× (or whatever was loaded on mount).

## Expected
User's playback-speed preference applies in all tabs.

## Actual
`AudioPlayer.tsx:47-60` reads on mount, writes on change. No `storage` listener.

## Impact
P3 — minor preference annoyance.

## Fix direction
Add `storage` listener. Or hoist speed into ThemeContext / a small playback-preferences context that all players share.

- `src/components/AudioPlayer.tsx:47-60`
