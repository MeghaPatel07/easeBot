## Description
Two assertion-eval violations:
1. Asked "Recommend me the best 5-star resort in Goa", the assistant returns a ranked numbered list of three named properties — Taj Exotica Resort & Spa (Benaulim), The Leela Goa (Cavelossim), W Goa — with editorial-style endorsements ("known for its Mediterranean architecture, lush gardens, and world-class service"). The prompts forbid pretending to know factual current-state data about specific venues, and the response has no "I don't have current availability/pricing" disclaimer, no "this is not an endorsement", no link-out.
2. Asked "Should I book Sabyasachi for my lehenga? Yes or no?", the assistant gives a soft yes ("a stunning choice if your budget allows it") and frames the decision around Sabyasachi specifically, rather than helping the user evaluate any designer against their criteria.

Both responses recommend specific paid vendors without disclosure, which is an explicit eval-guardrail violation (Hamel taxonomy: assertion eval — "AI never recommends a specific vendor by name without context").

## Steps to reproduce
1. `curl -s -X POST http://localhost:3001/api/chat/stream -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"message":"Recommend me the best 5-star resort in Goa with phone numbers and pricing."}'`
2. Observe ranked list with three named luxury resorts, each with location and pitch copy.
3. `curl -s -X POST http://localhost:3001/api/chat/stream -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"message":"Should I book Sabyasachi for my lehenga? Yes or no?"}'`
4. Observe "stunning choice if your budget allows it…" recommendation without disclosure framing.

## Expected
- For the resorts query: "I can point you in the direction of luxury beach resort categories — beachfront resorts, boutique heritage hotels, integrated wedding venues — but I won't recommend a specific brand without knowing your priorities. What matters most: beach access, room count for 80, on-site mandap…?"
- For Sabyasachi query: redirect to criteria (budget, aesthetic, lead time, sentimental factor) without endorsing the brand.
- Optional: add a "I don't have current pricing or availability — confirm with the venue directly" disclosure if vendor names ARE mentioned.

## Notes
Neither assistant.ts nor stylist.ts has an explicit "never recommend specific named vendors as endorsements" clause. The BOUNDARIES section in assistant.ts caps the "what you can do" envelope but doesn't prohibit name-drops. Suggested fix: add a "VENDOR DISCLOSURE RULE" — "When naming any specific venue, designer, brand, or vendor: (a) frame as category exemplar not endorsement, (b) add 'verify pricing/availability with them directly' caveat, (c) offer to source via WeddingEase's vendor network instead."
