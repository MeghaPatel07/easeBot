## Steps to reproduce
1. Open app. Send a message; load reminders; load checklists.
2. Switch to another browser tab. Leave for 6 minutes.
3. Switch back to easebot tab.

## Expected
Per TanStack default, `refetchOnWindowFocus: true` triggers refetch of stale queries.

## Actual
- `new QueryClient()` in App.tsx:53 uses ALL defaults, including `refetchOnWindowFocus: true`.
- BUT: only `useAccount` and `useUsageStats` route through Query. Reminders, checklists, threads, messages, saved items, notes, all use raw Firestore `onSnapshot` or one-shot `getDoc(s)`. Tab refocus refetches nothing for those domains.
- Worse: `useAccount` has `staleTime: 30_000` and `useUsageStats` has `staleTime: 30_000` + `refetchInterval: 60_000` — on focus the refetch may still hit cache if within stale window.
