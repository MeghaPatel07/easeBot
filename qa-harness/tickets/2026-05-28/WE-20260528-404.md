## Description

The Settings AI-Behavior tab exposes 10 tone sliders (warmth, cadence, etc.) and 6 voice presets, each preset carrying `rate`/`pitch`/`volume` values. Users reasonably expect the sliders to **change how the TTS speaks** (faster/slower, higher/lower).

In reality:
- `ttsController.handleTTS` receives `{text, voiceName, language}` ONLY — no rate/pitch/volume passed (`ttsController.ts:27`, `:66`).
- `azureTTS.generateSpeech` builds SSML without `<prosody rate=… pitch=… volume=…>` (`azureTTS.ts:101-105`).
- PRD-TTS-Pipeline §9 #9 documents this as a known limitation: "VoicePreset defines rate/pitch/volume but Gemini doesn't accept these" — but the product has since switched to Azure (see WE-20260528-013), which **does** accept SSML prosody. The migration didn't wire the sliders through.

Net effect: a user who moves the "Concise/Detailed" or "Calm/Enthusiastic" tone slider, or picks the "Echo (deep & confident)" preset over "Nova (bright & energetic)", will hear **identical output** apart from the voice timbre. Speaking rate, pitch, and emphasis are uniform across all presets.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Settings → AI Behavior. Set Aria preset, max out "Calm" slider, click Save.
2. Click Volume2 on any assistant message — note the cadence.
3. Switch to Nova preset, max out "Enthusiastic", save.
4. Click Volume2 again — same cadence, same pitch (voice timbre may differ slightly because Aria/Nova map to different Azure neural voices).

## Expected

- Wire `voicePreset.rate`/`pitch`/`volume` through to `azureTTS.generateSpeech` as SSML `<prosody>` attributes, OR
- Remove the rate/pitch/volume fields from `VoicePreset` and the "tone slider" labels that imply audio control. Document that tone sliders only affect text generation (not synthesis).

## Actual

- Saved to Firestore, never read by the TTS controller.
- Users believe they've personalized voice; system does nothing with the setting.

## Evidence

- `voicePresets.ts` — each preset carries `rate`/`pitch`.
- `ttsController.ts:27` destructures only `{text, voiceName, language}`.
- `azureTTS.ts:101-105` — SSML omits `<prosody>` wrapper.

## Notes

Compounds with WE-20260527-026 (tone-slider save-button state-sync). That ticket is about *saving* the sliders; this one is about whether the saved value does anything.
