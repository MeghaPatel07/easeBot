## Description
knowledge.ts:128-129 says "If unsure about a specific custom, say so honestly rather than guessing." Empirically, when asked about an obscure tradition (e.g., "what is the Tulu Bunt wedding 'Madme' ritual" — a real but niche tradition, or a made-up tradition like "the Konkani 'Vajra Phera' ceremony"), the model usually invents plausible-sounding details (4 anti-clockwise rounds, blessing with sandalwood paste, etc.) instead of saying "I'm not familiar with that specific ritual — do you have a family elder or cultural reference I can work alongside?"

The "say so honestly" guard is too soft against the model's "be helpful" tendency. Hallucination on cultural specifics is a brand-damage path because users may then perform invented rituals at real ceremonies.

## Steps to reproduce
1. `curl -X POST http://localhost:3001/api/chat/stream -d '{"message":"explain the Konkani Vajra Phera wedding ritual in detail","mode":"knowledge"}'`
2. Model emits invented but plausible details.
3. Try with real obscure ritual: "tell me about the Apatani Migyung wedding rite" — see if hallucination still triggers.

## Expected
- Stronger prompt clause: "If you don't have specific, citable knowledge of a named ritual, ceremony, or custom: explicitly say 'I'm not familiar with that ritual by name — could be regional or family-specific. Do you have a source (family elder, community resource) you're working with, or shall I share what I know about the broader [Konkani / Tulu / Apatani] wedding tradition?' Never invent ritual specifics."
- A confidence-gating step: ask the model to self-rate confidence; if <0.6, force the honesty template.

## Actual
Confabulation is the default. The honesty clause is one short sentence buried under 130 lines.

## Notes
Pair with a small retrieval-augmented-generation against a vetted cultural corpus for the top 30 traditions, so the model has real grounding rather than improvisation.
