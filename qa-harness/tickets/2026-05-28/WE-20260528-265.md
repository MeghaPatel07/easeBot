## Description

Deep scan shows `firstFocusable.tag: "DIV"` on all 6 audited pages — there is no skip-to-content link as the first interactive element. Keyboard users on `/login` (12 small links in header) and `/` (37+ small interactive elements) must Tab through the entire chrome before reaching primary content. WE-20260527-259 was filed yesterday — still `new` per ticket dir; reaffirming with broader page coverage.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open `/login`.
2. Press Tab once.
3. Focus lands on first header link, not a "Skip to content" affordance.

## Expected

First Tab reveals a visually-hidden-until-focused "Skip to main content" link pointing to `#main` (which requires the `<main>` landmark from WE-20260528-256).

## Actual

No skip link; users tab through up to 12 chrome elements per page.

## Notes

Sister-ticket to WE-20260527-259 and WE-20260528-256 — recommend bundle.
