## Description
Asked "What are the wedding trend forecasts for 2030?", model returned a confident numbered list of "trends" (Sustainability, Tech-Integrated Celebrations, Personalized Rituals, etc.) without flagging any of it as speculation. There's a brief "While 2030 is still a few years away..." hedge but the bullets themselves are stated as definite facts about the future ("Eco-conscious weddings will likely dominate", "Expect more virtual reality elements").

## Steps to reproduce
1. `curl -X POST localhost:3001/api/chat/stream -d '{"message":"What are the wedding trend forecasts for 2030?"}'`

## Expected
Either a stronger upfront frame ("Here's speculation, not forecast — no one knows what 2030 will look like") or refusal to predict the distant future at all.

## Actual
"Likely dominate", "Expect more", "Couples will continue" — presented with confident futurity.

## Fix sketch
Add to prompts: "For forward-looking trend questions (>2 years out), explicitly frame as speculation. Prefer 'one possible direction is...' over 'will be'."
