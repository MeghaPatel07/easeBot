## Description

Sprint 4 PostHog payload audit. Sprint 1 -242 flagged the **structural** sanitization gap; this is the full call-site audit.

`identify()` is called five times in `AuthContext.tsx` (signup-email, signin-email, google-signin, phone-signup, phone-signin) and the SECOND argument carries `email: firebaseUser.email ?? undefined`. PostHog stores email as a person property; once captured, it is indexed forever on that distinct_id and visible to anyone with project access. Consent banner is not gated against `identify()`.

There is **no allowlist or scrubber** on `identify` / `setUserProperties` / `register`. Compare to the documented contract in `docs/PostHog-EVENTS.md` (referenced in `analytics.ts:3`) — PII fields are not in the event taxonomy contract, but the wrapper exposes them via raw `properties: Record<string, unknown>` and the call sites send raw email.

Additional PII exposure surfaces:
- `setUserProperties({ signup_source, created_at })` at lines 154, 194 (low risk).
- `register({ is_authenticated, plan_tier })` at 126-129 (low risk).
- `register({ active_mode })` at `pages/Index.tsx:151` (low risk).

The **real bug** is email in `identify()`. Combined with `startReplay({ isPaying: planTier !== 'free' })` at line 130 (paying users record at 100%), a paying user's recordings + their email are joined.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Log in to `http://localhost:8081` with an email user.
2. Open DevTools → Network → filter `/decide` and `/e/?` (PostHog endpoints).
3. Inspect the body of the `$identify` event captured immediately after login.

## Expected

Either (a) only hashed/pseudonymous identifiers leave the browser, (b) `identify()` strips `email` and PostHog stores only a hash, or (c) the analytics consent banner explicitly enumerates "your email address is shared with our analytics provider" before any `identify` call.

## Actual

`distinct_id = firebase.uid` + `$set: { email: 'real-user@example.com', plan_tier: 'free' }` go out on every auth-state change. Tab B / page refresh re-emits.

## Notes

Fix-frontend: scrub the `properties` param in `identify()` against an allowlist (`plan_tier`, `signup_source`, `created_at`, NOT email/name/phone/nickname). If email is needed for cohorts, send `email_domain` only.
