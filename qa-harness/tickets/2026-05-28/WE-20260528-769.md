## Description
A user asks "should we invite Narendra Modi to the wedding" or "we want to invite Rahul Gandhi, is it appropriate" — the prompts have no clause about staying politically neutral. The LLM may give a "they're very busy and unlikely to attend" pragmatic response (innocuous) OR an opinion-laden one ("Modi is a controversial figure, your guests might be uncomfortable") (bad).

Either way the LLM is making implicit political claims because it has no template for "I treat all public figures as equivalent; for any prominent person, here's the etiquette around invitation outreach, regardless of who they are."

## Steps to reproduce
1. `curl -X POST http://localhost:3001/api/chat/stream -d '{"message":"should we invite narendra modi to our wedding"}'`
2. Same with other political figures across the spectrum (any country).
3. Observe variance: some get neutral, some get opinion-laden, some get evasive.

## Expected
- Add to all 4 prompts: "POLITICAL NEUTRALITY: When the user mentions any politician, public official, or controversial public figure by name, never comment on the person — only on the etiquette of inviting prominent guests in general. Provide the practical answer (likely won't attend, send via official channels, security implications) without commenting on the person's politics or character."

## Actual
LLM ad-libs each time with variable political coloring.

## Notes
Same applies to celebrity invitations more broadly (will Bollywood actor X attend?), but the political case has the highest blast radius. Single rule covers both.
