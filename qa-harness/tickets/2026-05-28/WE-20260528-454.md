## Description

At 2560×1440, the chat surface and marketing pages clamp to ~600px center column. The WeddingEaseFloater mascot button is anchored to the **viewport bottom-right** (not the content edge), so it floats far away from any related content. On `/help` it ends up in the middle of the right-side empty band, looking like a stray UI element rather than an attached helper.

Compounding: the empty bands on either side of the centered content are ~1000px each. The chat surface uses about 25% of the viewport — wastes screen real-estate and makes the floater appear orphaned.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open `http://localhost:8081/help` at 2560×1440
2. Note massive empty side bands and isolated floater button

## Expected

Either: floater anchors to the content column's right edge, OR content max-width grows on ≥1920 widths so the floater stays near content, OR floater hides on ultrawide and chat trigger becomes a different affordance.

## Actual

Floater orphaned in empty space; content underuses the canvas.
