## Description
Cultural-humility eval: asked about Khasi wedding tradition "Ka Pynhiar Synjat", the model produced confident, specific claims ("translates to 'the tying of the betel leaf'", "matrilineal structure of Khasi society") with zero hedging. This is a low-resource cultural concept where the model is at high risk of fabricating plausible-sounding detail. There is no marker like "I'm not certain — please verify with a community elder."

## Steps to reproduce
1. `curl -X POST localhost:3001/api/chat/stream -d '{"message":"Can you explain the Khasi wedding tradition of Ka Pynhiar Synjat?"}'`
2. Observe confident multi-paragraph description with no hedges.

## Expected
Per WeddingEase's "cultural humility" goal, response should signal uncertainty for non-mainstream traditions: "I have limited information on this specific Khasi ritual; what I share is from general sources and you may want to verify with a Khasi community elder."

## Actual
Confident encyclopedic answer; no uncertainty markers; high fabrication risk.

## Fix sketch
Add to all mode prompts: "For obscure cultural traditions, regional dialects, or low-resource topics, signal uncertainty explicitly. Prefer 'in some traditions...' / 'one common version is...' over definitive claims."
