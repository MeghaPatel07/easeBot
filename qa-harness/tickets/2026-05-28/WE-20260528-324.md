## Description

Lighthouse resource-summary on /chat shows 41 requests: 20 Script, 4 Image, 12 Other, 3 Stylesheet, 2 Document. 13 are third-party.

Many of the 20 script requests are tiny single-icon chunks (sparkles-BOlchDha.js 0.68KB, plus-cmuY856_.js 0.56KB, heart-C5b0w5zd.js 1.72KB, message-square-Bs_-iCZt.js 0.35KB etc.) — Vite has over-split icon imports into their own chunks. On HTTP/2 this is acceptable, but on bandwidth-constrained mobile each request still pays a TLS+round-trip cost.

## Steps to reproduce

1. View lighthouse network-requests
2. ls dist/assets/*.js | wc -l

## Expected

≤ 15 requests on initial load.

## Actual

41 requests, including ~12 sub-1KB icon chunks.
