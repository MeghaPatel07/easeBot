## Description

The currency dropdown at Pricing.tsx:425-440 is a normal `<select>` with no `data-ph-mask` / `data-ph-block`. With `analytics.ts:191` `maskAllInputs: true`, *values* of inputs are masked — but `<select>` chosen-option text is rendered into the DOM, captured in the snapshot stream.

Per `startReplay`, 10% of users on `/pricing` are recorded. Their geo-currency override choice is visible to anyone viewing the replay (it's not PII per GDPR Article 4, but combined with IP-geo it is location-revealing). 

Same for the occasion chip selection (Engagement / Haldi / etc.) at Index.tsx — the chip choice is in the snapshot.

## Expected

Add `data-ph-mask` to the currency `<select>` and to user-choice chip clusters that reveal location/event-context.

## Actual

Snapshotted as-is.

## Notes

Low risk but a documented gap. Fix-frontend.
