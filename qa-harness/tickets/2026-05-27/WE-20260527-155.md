## Steps to reproduce
1. Sign in.
2. Note "Liked" badge count in sidebar (ChatSidebar.tsx:354 uses `allLikedMessagesCount`).
3. In chat, click thumbs-up to like an assistant message.
4. Watch sidebar badge + Liked view.

## Expected
Badge increments immediately; Liked view shows the new message.

## Actual
- `useChat.ts` lines 215-255: `allLikedMessages` is fetched with `getDocs` ONCE keyed on `user?.uid`. Never re-runs unless user logs out and back in.
- Likes happen via `toggleLikeMessage` (chatService.ts:151) — writes to Firestore but doesn't push to `allLikedMessages`.
- Until refresh, the badge stays the same. Liked view shows old set.

## Impact
P1 — feature visibly broken; badge appears to lie.

## Fix direction
Replace `getDocs` with a `collectionGroup` query + `onSnapshot` subscription. Or optimistically push into local state after toggleLikeMessage.

## Evidence
- src/hooks/useChat.ts:215-255 (one-shot fetch)
- src/services/chatService.ts:151-161 (toggle without listener)
- src/components/chat/ChatSidebar.tsx:354 (consumer of stale badge count)
