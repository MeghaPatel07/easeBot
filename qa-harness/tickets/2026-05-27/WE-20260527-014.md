## Description

Clicking "Continue With Email" without first entering an email produces only a tiny top-of-card pink banner: "Please enter your email address". The email input itself is **not** marked invalid, has no red ring, no `aria-invalid`, no descriptive error tied via `aria-describedby`. Screen-reader users get nothing. Sighted users may miss the banner.

## Steps to reproduce

1. `http://localhost:8081/login`.
2. Dismiss consent.
3. Click "Continue With Email" without typing anything.

## Expected

Email field shows an inline error: red ring, `aria-invalid="true"`, `aria-describedby` pointing to a `role="alert"` text node ("Please enter your email address"). Focus moves to the field.

## Actual

Only the small banner at the top of the card. Field is unmarked.
