## Description

The Help page shows:
> Company Details:
> Subh Wedding Ease Private Limited
> Contact: +91 99250 74485 | theweddingease@gmail.com

Both the phone number and email are rendered as plain text, not as `<a href="tel:...">` or `<a href="mailto:...">`. Tap-to-call on mobile and click-to-compose on desktop don't work. With `<meta name="format-detection" content="telephone=no">` set in `index.html`, even iOS auto-link detection is suppressed — so the user really cannot dial from the page.

## Steps to reproduce

1. `http://localhost:8081/help` on mobile.
2. Tap the phone number → nothing happens.
3. Tap the email → nothing happens.

## Expected

`<a href="tel:+919925074485">+91 99250 74485</a>` and `<a href="mailto:theweddingease@gmail.com">theweddingease@gmail.com</a>`.

## Actual

Plain text.

## Evidence

- `qa-pages.mjs` help body dump.

## Notes

`index.html` `format-detection=telephone=no` is a deliberate iOS opt-out — keep it but provide explicit `tel:` links.
