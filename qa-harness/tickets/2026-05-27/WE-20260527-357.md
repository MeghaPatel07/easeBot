## Description
modeRouter scoring is a simple sum of regex match counts. When the user message contains both a stylist-noun ("mangalsutra", "lehenga", "saree") AND a knowledge stem ("what is the meaning", "explain"), stylist's two-regex coverage (wedding nouns + Indian attire/jewellery nouns) racks up more matches than knowledge's single broad-question regex. Knowledge intent loses the tie.

This is a softer variant of WE-20260527-352 — the underlying mechanism is the same: per-pattern count beats intent-shape.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Several test queries (all returned stylist):
   - "What is the meaning of the mangalsutra in Hindu weddings?" → stylist ❌ (expected knowledge)
   - "Tell me about the history of the lehenga in Indian weddings" → stylist (expected knowledge)
2. Compare to clean knowledge classification:
   - "What is the meaning of Haldi ceremony" → knowledge ✓

## Expected
When a knowledge-stem phrase appears AND the noun is wedding-domain, prefer knowledge mode.

## Actual
Stylist wins on noun coverage alone.
