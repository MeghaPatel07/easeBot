## Description

The guest homepage shows the same call to action twice within ~50 px of each other:
- Hero: "Get Responses Tailored To You — Log in to save chats, create images, and get personalized wedding planning. [Log In]"
- Below: "Guest Mode — 10 messages remaining · 3 images remaining — This chat won't be saved. **Sign in to save your conversations.**"

Two CTAs to "sign in to save" overlap in meaning. The duplication is visible in every body-text dump.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open `http://localhost:8081/` as guest.
2. Read the top of the page.

## Expected

A single concise sign-in CTA — either consolidate the messaging into one banner or remove the redundant tagline.

## Actual

Two CTAs side by side. Slight wording difference but same intent.
