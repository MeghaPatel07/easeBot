## Description
modeRouter.ts classifies the question "What is the meaning of the mangalsutra in Hindu weddings?" as `stylist` mode because the keyword "necklace" / "mangalsutra" matches the stylist jewellery regex on line 32. The user is clearly asking a cultural-knowledge question ("meaning of"), and the knowledge regex on line 38 (which matches "meaning") loses the scoring tie. The model then opens the response in stylist voice and closes with "Would you like styling advice for incorporating the mangalsutra into your bridal look?" instead of explaining the cultural significance.

## Steps to reproduce
1. `curl -X POST http://localhost:3001/api/chat -d '{"message":"What is the meaning of the mangalsutra in Hindu weddings?"}'`
2. Observe `mode=stylist` in response.

## Expected
`mode=knowledge` since the question literally uses "What is the meaning of …".

## Actual
`mode=stylist`. Trailing question pivots to styling advice rather than tradition/meaning follow-up.

## Evidence
- `/Users/krish/Desktop/easebot/qa-harness/evidence/WE-20260527-352/knowledge_1.json`

## Notes
Either (a) re-weight scoring so "what is the meaning of" beats single noun matches, (b) prefer the longest-prefix question pattern, or (c) move all the wedding-attire nouns out of stylist when paired with a "what is" / "meaning" / "history" query stem.
