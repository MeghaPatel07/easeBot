The pricing page renders eight currency pill buttons (USD, INR, GBP, EUR, AED, SGD, AUD, CAD). Body text shows them rendered as plain buttons. They likely use a visual selected state via Tailwind classes but no `aria-pressed="true"` or `aria-current` to convey the active currency to assistive tech.

## Steps to reproduce

1. `http://localhost:8081/pricing`.
2. Inspect each currency button's attributes.
