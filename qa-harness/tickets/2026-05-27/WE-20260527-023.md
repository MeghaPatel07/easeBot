## Description

The 404 page reads:
> 404
> Oops! Page not found
> [Return to Home]

That's it. Common typos (`/privacy-policy` instead of `/privacy`, `/terms-of-service` instead of `/terms`, `/images` instead of `/{userId}/images`, `/settings` instead of `/?settings=account`) all land here with zero guidance. A 404 in 2026 should offer at least a search and the most-visited pages.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Visit `http://localhost:8081/privacy-policy`, `/terms-of-service`, `/images`, `/settings`, or any other unsupported URL.

## Expected

A 404 page with quick links: Home, Sign in, Pricing, Help/FAQ.

## Actual

Bare-bones 404. The four URLs above all hit this dead end — see WE-20260527-024 for the deeper deeplink issue.

## Evidence

- Body text in `qa-pages.mjs`.

## Notes

If preserving the minimalist aesthetic, at minimum add "Pricing | Help | Login" links beneath "Return to Home".
