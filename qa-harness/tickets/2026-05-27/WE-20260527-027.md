## Description

The Voice section lists six presets (Aria, Echo, Nova, Vale, Luna, Sol) with the copy "Tap the speaker to preview." There's a "Save voice" button at the bottom. We could not find a speaker icon in the body-text dump — the icon may be SVG-only without aria-label. A blind user has no way to preview. Sighted users have no indication of which preset is currently selected vs which is being previewed.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Settings → AI Behavior → Voice.
2. Try to find the "speaker" preview icon for "Aria".
3. Aria-enumerate buttons in this region.

## Expected

Each voice card has a clearly labelled speaker button (`aria-label="Preview Aria voice"`). The current voice has a distinct selected state. Saving toasts the change.

## Actual

Speaker icons exist as SVG-only buttons (no aria label). Selection state and save confirmation are ambiguous in screenshots.

## Evidence

- Settings body dump in `qa-settings.mjs`: no "speaker", "preview", or "play" aria labels listed in the area.

Tied to PRD-TTS-Pipeline §3 (the synthesis flow). Confirm Gemini TTS endpoint is hit on preview.
