## Description
Knowledge mode system prompt mandates "Keep responses 2-4 lines. Short sentences only. One topic at a time. Never dump a full encyclopedia entry." In practice, knowledge responses regularly exceed this, returning 5+ non-empty lines, 1000+ chars with subsections like "WHAT HAPPENS / WHAT TO WEAR / WHAT IS NEEDED".

## Steps to reproduce
1. `curl -X POST http://localhost:3001/api/chat -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"message":"How does a Sikh Anand Karaj ceremony work?"}'`
2. Observe response: 5 non-empty lines, 1295 chars, structured into 3 numbered subsections.

## Expected
2-4 lines, single topic.
