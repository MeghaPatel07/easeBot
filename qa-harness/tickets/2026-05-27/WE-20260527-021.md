## Description

Direct visit to `/payment/success` (no PayU token in URL, no session) renders:
> Payment received
> We could not verify your transaction (You were signed out during checkout. Please sign in again to view this receipt.). Please contact support if this persists.
> [Go to Plan & Billing] [Back to app]

The headline "Payment received" then immediately tells the user it cannot verify the transaction. Contradictory: either the payment IS received and verified, or it isn't.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open incognito at `http://localhost:8081/payment/success`.
2. Read the page.

## Expected

If there is no payment token + no auth, show a neutral "Sign in to view payment receipt" page; do not lead with "Payment received".

## Actual

Mixed message — could mislead users into thinking they were charged when they weren't (or panic users into contacting support when they shouldn't).

## Evidence

- Body text in `qa-pages.mjs`.

## Notes

Trust-impacting copy in the payment funnel.
