## Steps to reproduce
1. Phone-based account: Settings → change display email via `updateProfile({ email })` (AccountTab line 261).
2. Watch profile menu (line 180 — `profile.email`), NotesView (Index.tsx:1350 passes `profile.email`), InvitePartner (line 1349).

## Expected
All surfaces show new email within ~1s.

## Actual
- ProfileMenu uses `useAccount.profile` — DOES update.
- NotesView/InvitePartner use `useAuth().profile.email` — STALE until reload.

## Impact
P1 — split sources of truth. Two surfaces showing different emails in the same window.

## Fix direction
Same as WE-20260527-150 — AuthContext.onSnapshot to unify both consumers.

## Evidence
- src/components/ProfileMenu.tsx:180 (useAccount — fresh)
- src/pages/Index.tsx:1349-1350 (useAuth — stale)
