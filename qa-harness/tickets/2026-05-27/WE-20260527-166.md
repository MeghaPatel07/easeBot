## Steps to reproduce
1. Sign up as new user.
2. Send first chat message.
3. Without reload, navigate to another route or trigger the onboarding gate check.

## Expected
Onboarding considered complete.

## Actual
- Backend marks onboarding complete on first message. Frontend `profile.onboardingComplete` (or equivalent) lives in AuthContext.
- AuthContext doesn't subscribe; staying on the session, the field remains `false`.
- If a modal/route guard checks `profile.onboardingComplete`, it re-shows onboarding until reload.

## Impact
P2 — bothers new users specifically; first impression cost.

## Fix direction
Same root: AuthContext onSnapshot. Or, after first message send, explicitly invalidate the account/me query AND ensure consumers read from useAccount.

## Evidence
- General pattern; consumers of `profile.*` from AuthContext (see WE-20260527-150 evidence list).

## Note (2026-05-27)
Likely closed by WE-20260527-170 PR — verify after merge. When the backend marks onboarding complete on first message, the Firestore write fans through the new AuthContext snapshot listener and `profile.onboardingComplete` updates without F5; route guards reading from `useAuth()` see the fresh value.
