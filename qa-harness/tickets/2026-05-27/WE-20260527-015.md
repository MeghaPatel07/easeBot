## Description

Clicking an occasion chip (e.g. "Engagement") updates internal state (`selectedOccasion`) and enables the reset button, but the chip's visual change (`bg-primary/20 border-primary/40 text-primary`) is so subtle that, in screenshots captured at 1440×900, the selected chip is visually indistinguishable from the unselected ones. The body-text dump shows no visible change either.

## Steps to reproduce

1. `http://localhost:8081/`, dismiss consent.
2. Click "Engagement" in the occasion strip.
3. Take a screenshot before vs after.

## Expected

Selected chip has obvious filled background, a label badge ("Engagement selected"), or moves to the front so the chip is unmistakably "on".

## Actual

`bg-primary/20` at 20% alpha on top of a beige background is barely a tint. Confidence in selection state is low.

## Evidence

- Screenshot pair: `/tmp/qa-modes-empty.png` vs `/tmp/qa-engagement-selected.png` (visually near-identical).

## Notes

Increase the alpha to 40-50% or add a check icon next to the label.
