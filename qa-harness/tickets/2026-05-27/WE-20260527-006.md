## Description

`AnalyticsConsent.tsx` is rendered as a `fixed bottom-4 left-1/2 z-[100]` dialog with `role="dialog"`. The dialog **intercepts pointer events** on the area below it, including the chat textarea/send button (both ChatInput and the language menu trigger). Until the user clicks Accept or Decline, the chat composer is effectively unclickable in the overlap region. Playwright's auto-retry click hits the consent dialog as the topmost element and times out after 30 s with "subtree intercepts pointer events".

## Steps to reproduce

1. Clear `localStorage` (`localStorage.clear()`).
2. Reload `http://localhost:8081/` — the banner appears at bottom-center.
3. Try to click the chat textarea or the language-menu chip in the overlap zone *without* dismissing the banner.

## Expected

Either (a) the banner does not block interactions below it (use `pointer-events:none` on the wrapper, `pointer-events:auto` on the inner card), or (b) the consent banner is repositioned so it never overlaps interactive elements.

## Actual

Pointer events are blocked. Click locator targeting `textarea` says: "element is visible, enabled and stable" but `<div … aria-label="Analytics consent" …> subtree intercepts pointer events". Users must dismiss the banner to type their first message.

## Evidence

- Playwright failure transcript captured in `qa-chat.mjs` run (timeout after 30 s on the textarea click).

## Notes

Even after the click is dispatched, the language-menu button stays "not visible" while the consent overlay is still open (see WE-20260527-013 below). A simple `style.pointerEvents = 'none'` on the wrapper plus `pointer-events:auto` on the inner card fixes both problems.
