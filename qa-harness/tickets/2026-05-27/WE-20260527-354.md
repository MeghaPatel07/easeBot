## Description
In a 10-turn conversation with history threaded between turns, the model:
1. **Self-contradicts on duration**: Turn 2 (planner) computes "about 6.5 months away" for Dec 15 2026 from today (2026-05-27) — correct. Turn 7 (assistant) computes "about 38 months away" for the same date — wildly wrong.
2. **Forgets the user's identity**: User opens Turn 1 with "Hi I'm Priya, my wedding is on December 15th 2026". The model correctly recalls "Priya" in Turn 4 and "Dec 15, 2026" in Turn 5. But by Turn 10, asked "What did I tell you about myself in the first message?", model replies "You didn't share anything personal about yourself yet, but I'd love to know more!" — directly contradicting Turns 4 and 5.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Run `/tmp/run_trajectory2.py` (saved as evidence).
2. Inspect Turn 7 vs Turn 2 for the months-away contradiction.
3. Inspect Turn 10 — model claims user shared nothing personal.

## Expected
- Consistent date arithmetic across turns (always ~7 months).
- At Turn 10, model recalls "You mentioned you're Priya and your wedding is December 15th 2026."

## Actual
- Turn 2: "about 6.5 months away"
- Turn 7: "about 38 months away"  ← contradicts Turn 2
- Turn 10: "You didn't share anything personal" ← contradicts Turn 1, 4, 5

## Notes
Both failures are arithmetic/reference-resolution drift, not vanilla forgetting — the assistant prompt at line 76-79 explicitly says "Remember what was discussed earlier in this conversation. Reference it naturally. If the user returns to a topic, pick up where you left off — don't restart." The model is silently ignoring this when the question is phrased indirectly ("What did I tell you about myself"). Consider (a) adding a system reminder of the user-shared facts at the head of every turn, or (b) tightening max conversation summary handoff in the conversationSummarizer service.
