## Description

The Free card shows "$0 / month" followed by "Billed in INR. ~$0 USD." For a free plan, the "Billed in INR" line is meaningless and the "~$0 USD" is comical. The same micro-copy applies usefully to Pro/Pro Max — but for Free it should be suppressed.

## Steps to reproduce

1. `http://localhost:8081/pricing`.
2. Read the Free card.

## Expected

Free card omits the "Billed in INR" sub-line, or replaces it with "No payment required".

## Actual

`Free / $0 / month / Billed in INR. ~$0 USD.`
