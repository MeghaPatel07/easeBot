## Description

The fullscreen image preview is rendered as a `<div>` overlay, NOT as a Radix Dialog. It lacks:
- `role="dialog"` + `aria-modal="true"`
- Focus trap (Tab can escape the lightbox to background content)
- Escape-to-close binding
- Focus return to the originating thumbnail when the overlay closes

Same issue may affect NotesView.tsx image-zoom (line 482) and SavedItemsView.tsx (245).

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open an AI-generated image in the chat
2. Press Tab repeatedly

## Expected

Focus stays inside the lightbox; Escape closes; focus returns to the thumbnail.

## Actual

Focus walks out of the overlay into the chat behind; Escape has no effect on the lightbox.

## Notes

Migrate the lightbox to `Dialog` from `@radix-ui/react-dialog` (already in use elsewhere).
