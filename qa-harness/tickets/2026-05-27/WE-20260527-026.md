## Description

The AI Behavior tab renders 10 named tone sliders (Warm/Cool, Analytical/Intuitive, Friendly/Formal, Professional/Casual, Enthusiastic/Calm, Concise/Detailed, Quirky/Straight, Candid/Diplomatic, Emojis None/Lots, Headers Prose/Structured). Below them is a single "Save tone" button. There's no indication of "unsaved changes" — a user who tweaks sliders, switches tabs, and comes back may lose them or be surprised by them. Modern settings UI either autosaves or makes the "dirty" state obvious (badge, sticky bar with "Save / Discard").

## Steps to reproduce

1. Settings → AI Behavior.
2. Move "Friendly/Formal" slider.
3. Click "Data & Privacy" tab without pressing "Save tone".
4. Return to AI Behavior tab.

## Expected

Either autosave on slider release, or persistent "Unsaved changes — Save / Discard" sticky banner.

## Actual

No dirty indicator. User must remember to press "Save tone" or risk losing the change.

## Evidence

- Settings body dump in `qa-settings.mjs` shows the layout.

## Notes

Combined with 30 `[role="slider"]` elements (10 sliders × 3 thumbs?) — verify the count is correct in code.
