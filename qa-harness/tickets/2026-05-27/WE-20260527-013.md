## Description

Trying to click `button[aria-label="Response language"]` when the AnalyticsConsent banner is still showing returns "Element is not visible". The consent overlay sits at the bottom but its `z-[100]` plus its body wrapper apparently elevates an offscreen flex chain that shadows the language pill. (See related WE-20260527-006.)

## Steps to reproduce

1. Clear localStorage to force consent banner.
2. Reload `http://localhost:8081/`.
3. With banner still open, try to programmatically click `button[aria-label="Response language"]`.
4. Repeats: "element is not visible".

## Expected

Header controls always interactive regardless of unrelated overlays.

## Actual

Header buttons report `isVisible: false` until the banner is dismissed.

## Evidence

- `qa-deep2.mjs` reports `LangBtn: true` then `Element is not visible` on click attempt.

## Notes

Likely a `pointer-events`/z-index regression. Fix overlaps with WE-20260527-006.
