## Description
Sending 15 concurrent chat requests yields 5 × 429 + 10 × 200 (the limiter works). However the 429 responses do not include a `Retry-After` header. The body is a brief `{"error":"Too many requests, please try again later."}` with no machine-readable hint of when the limit refills. Clients have to guess.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Fire 15 concurrent `POST /api/chat` requests.
2. Inspect headers on the 429 responses — no `Retry-After`, no `X-RateLimit-Reset`, no `RateLimit-Reset`.

## Expected
- 429 includes `Retry-After: <seconds>` (RFC 6585) or `RateLimit-Reset: <seconds>` (IETF draft).
- Body includes a structured timing field, e.g. `{"error":"rate_limited","retryAfterSec":N}`.

## Actual
429 with plain English message, no retry hint.

## Notes
`express-rate-limit` exposes `standardHeaders: true` and `legacyHeaders: false` options that emit RFC-compliant headers automatically. Likely a one-line config change in `middleware/rateLimiter.ts`.
