## Description

The Nickname `<label>` and `<input>` are not linked via `htmlFor`/`id`. The helper-text `<p>` containing instructions ("What should the AI call you during conversations?") is also not connected via `aria-describedby`. SR users hear only "edit text" with no field label.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open Settings → Identity tab
2. Tab to the Nickname field with VoiceOver

## Expected

"Nickname, edit text — What should the AI call you during conversations?"

## Actual

"edit text" — no name, no description.

## Notes

Fix: `id="nickname"`, `htmlFor="nickname"`, `aria-describedby="nickname-help"` on the helper paragraph.
