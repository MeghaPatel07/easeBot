## Description

The Feedback dialog body shows:
> Your feedback * 0 / 5000

The asterisk denotes required, but the textarea is not `required` / `aria-required="true"`. Submitting empty likely does nothing without telling the user why.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open Send Feedback dialog.
2. Click "Send Feedback" without typing.

## Expected

`aria-required="true"` on the textarea, and on submit-empty, an inline error appears below the field.

## Actual

The "*" implies required; the input is not flagged as such programmatically.

- `qa-aux.mjs` FEEDBACK_BODY dump.
