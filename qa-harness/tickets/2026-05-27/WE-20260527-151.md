## Steps to reproduce
1. Sign in as `free` tier user.
2. In Tab A, complete a PayU upgrade flow (or have backend bump `plan` to `pro`).
3. In Tab B (Profile menu open), watch the "FREE/PRO/PRO MAX" pill.

## Expected
Pill flips to "PRO" within ~1s of backend confirmation.

## Actual
- TokenPoolBar/useTokenPool depend on `useUsageStats` which has `refetchInterval: 60_000` and `staleTime: 30_000` (src/hooks/useUsageStats.ts:7-8).
- `ProfileMenu` line 149 computes tier from `plan?.tier ?? profile.plan` — `plan` comes from `useAccount` (`staleTime: 30_000` in useAccount.ts:81) and `profile.plan` from stale `AuthContext`.
- Worst-case delay: up to 60s before any UI consumer sees the new tier.
- Upgrade CTAs ("Upgrade plan" line 219) keep showing for up to 60s after user paid.

## Impact
P1 — user just paid; sees "Free" + upgrade prompts. Erodes trust.

## Fix direction
After upgrade flow returns 200, optimistically `qc.setQueryData(['account','me'], …)` AND `qc.invalidateQueries(['account','usage'])`. Backend can also push via a Firestore field with onSnapshot.

## Evidence
- src/hooks/useUsageStats.ts:7-18 (60s refetchInterval)
- src/hooks/useAccount.ts:81 (30s staleTime)
- src/components/ProfileMenu.tsx:149,219 (consumer)
