WE-20260528-1051 - autoComplete attribute audit
================================================

BEFORE (origin/Bug-Resolve-claude, pre-fix)
--------------------------------------------
Total autoComplete occurrences across the three target files: 0

  src/components/auth/SignInModal.tsx ...... 0
  src/components/auth/SignUpModal.tsx ...... 0
  src/components/auth/PhoneInput.tsx ....... 0

Result: Every email, password, OTP, name, and phone input in the
auth modals rendered without an autocomplete attribute. Password
managers (1Password, Bitwarden, iCloud Keychain) could not autofill
credentials. WCAG 2.2 Level AA SC 3.3.8 + SC 1.3.5 fail.

AFTER (this patch)
------------------
Total autoComplete occurrences across the three target files: 18

  src/components/auth/SignInModal.tsx ...... 9
  src/components/auth/SignUpModal.tsx ...... 8
  src/components/auth/PhoneInput.tsx ....... 1
