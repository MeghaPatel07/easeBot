Bug summary

VibeDNAStrip imposes maxLength={30} on its new-descriptor input; CustomVibeForm imposes maxLength={40} on its title input. Both silently stop accepting input at the cap with no UI counter, leaving users to wonder why the field stopped responding. PersonalizationTab already has the correct pattern ({value.length} / {MAX} sibling). PR #74 set an in-app convention of showing the counter only within ~10 of the cap and switching to a warning color at the cap.
