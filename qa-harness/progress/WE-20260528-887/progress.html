Bug summary

On mobile/tablet, the "All notes" picker popover acts modal-like. Clicking a different note in the picker triggers handleSelectNote(noteId) (sets activeNoteId) BEFORE closing the popover (setNotesPickerOpen(false)). This produces a one-frame race where the previous note's editor content can render under the new note's title because the TipTap editor's useEffect([activeNoteId]) content reset is async.
