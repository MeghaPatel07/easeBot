WE-20260527-052 · Mobile chat input below WCAG 44px touch target P1

Repo: Wedding-Ease-Viva-Chat · Agent: fix-frontend · Branch: fix-WE-20260527-052 → Bug-Resolve-claude · Worktree: /Users/krish/Desktop/easebot/worktrees/fix-WE-20260527-052

Bug summary

On mobile (375px), the primary chat textarea rendered at 251×37px and the surrounding mic/send icon buttons at ~40×32px — all under the WCAG 2.5.5 / iOS HIG minimum touch target of 44×44px. Result: missed taps land on background or adjacent buttons. The fix raises the textarea to min-h-[44px] on mobile (desktop unchanged at 36px) and bumps all five icon buttons (cancel, send-voice, mic, stop, send) from h-10 w-10 to h-11 w-11 on mobile while preserving the desktop sm:h-9 sm:w-9 compact look.
