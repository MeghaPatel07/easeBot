WE-20260527-047 · Backend rate-limit (HTTP 429) lacks Retry-After header P2

Repo: easebot-backend · Agent: fix-backend · Branch: Bug-Resolve-claude · Worktree: worktrees/fix-WE-20260527-047

Bug summary

On 429 from POST /api/chat/stream (and other /api/* routes) the response had no machine-readable back-off info — no Retry-After, no RateLimit-* headers, and an opaque body ("Too many requests, please try again later."). Clients couldn't back off intelligently and the SDK could not render a "slow down" UX.
