Bug summary

In src/pages/settings/tabs/AccountTab.tsx, handleSaveIdentity catches the mutation rejection and surfaces a single generic toast: "Could not save" / err.message ?? "Please try again.". This collapses five distinct failure shapes (client validation, backend validation, 401 auth-expired, 429 rate-limit, 5xx server, network drop) into one message — users can't tell whether to fix their input, re-sign-in, wait, or retry.
