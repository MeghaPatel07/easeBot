WE-20260528-107 · sharedChats threadTitle not sanitized — Markdown/HTML can render in public view P2

Repo: Wedding-Ease-Viva-Chat · Agent: fix-frontend · Branch: fix-WE-20260528-107 → Bug-Resolve-claude · Worktree: worktrees/fix-WE-20260528-107

Bug summary

createSharedChat(threadId, threadTitle) in src/services/chatService.ts wrote threadTitle verbatim into the public sharedChats Firestore doc, with zero sanitization. The /share/:shareId page surfaced that title in the header. A malicious user could rename a thread to [Pwned](javascript:alert(1)) or <img src=x onerror=alert(1)> and ship a public link that injects untrusted Markdown/HTML into a third party’s browsing session. React’s text-node escaping protects against raw <script>, but Markdown-link syntax + javascript: URIs slip through any future Markdown-renderer regression.
