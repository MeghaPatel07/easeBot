WE-20260527-021 · PaymentSuccess shows "could not verify your transaction" for unauthenticated users P2

Repo: Wedding-Ease-Viva-Chat · Agent: fix-frontend · Branch: fix-WE-20260527-021 (PRs into Bug-Resolve-claude) · Worktree: worktrees/fix-WE-20260527-021

Bug summary

Direct visit to /payment/success with no auth session renders the headline "Payment received" followed by a destructive error message "We could not verify your transaction". Contradictory + trust-impacting copy in the payment funnel. Either the user was charged (and we just can't show it) or they weren't — mixing the two confuses users and risks panic-driven support tickets.

Audit trail

2026-05-29 — start
Read ticket + source files. Confirmed root cause: PaymentSuccess always renders "Payment received" headline regardless of state, then uses error string set during auth-state hydration to drive a destructive sub-line.
Root cause located in src/pages/PaymentSuccess.tsx — the unauthenticated branch (onAuthStateChanged with no user) sets error via the same channel as real verify failures, and the JSX has no state-based headline switch.
2026-05-29 — fix
Introduced an explicit authState: 'pending' | 'in' | 'out' + separated "needs sign-in" from real verify errors. When no user, show neutral copy ("Sign in to view your payment receipt") with an in-page SignInModal trigger, preserve txnid in sessionStorage, and keep the user on the page (no auto-redirect). After sign-in, the auth-state listener re-fires and resumes verification.
Headline now reads "Payment received" only when the verify succeeds (info.state === 'paid'), "Verifying your payment…" while in flight, "Sign in to view your payment receipt" when unauthenticated, and "We couldn't verify your transaction" on a real failure. Pending txnid is restored from sessionStorage if the URL is missing it (defensive — handles the "user signed in then refreshed without query string" sub-case).
