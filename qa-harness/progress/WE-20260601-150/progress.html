Bug summary

WE-20260601-150: 9 occurrences of invalid double-opacity Tailwind classes (text-foreground/90/90, text-foreground/90/85) in Help.tsx (8) and CapHitBanner.tsx (1). A Tailwind color utility accepts at most ONE opacity modifier, so the JIT emits no rule and the element silently falls back to its inherited color (a contrast/consistency risk in both themes; the Back-link hover never fires).

WE-20260601-355: The public SharedChat header used bg-foreground/80 with text-foreground/80 title and text-foreground/40 metadata. Background and text both derive from --foreground, producing white-on-white (dark theme) / dark-on-dark (light theme) far below WCAG AA 4.5:1. This is a public, unauthenticated, shareable page.
