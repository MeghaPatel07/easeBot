Bundles ruling for WE-20260528-1098 (Unusual Words, P3) + WE-20260528-1057 (Reading Level, P2). RULING: keep cultural terms + add inline tooltip/glossary.

Bug summary

Vibe-preset titles, subtitles and descriptor chips use specialist / cultural vocabulary (jaali, jharokha, brocade, zari, filigree, damask, chiaroscuro, monstera, heliconia, macrame, pampas, lehenga, sherwani, …) that renders in the /images vibe picker, the active-vibe DNA strip, and Settings → Personalization with no inline explanation. For ESL, cognitively-impaired and culturally-distant users these words are opaque (WCAG 3.1.3 Unusual Words). Per the ruling the words are kept — they are intentional and meaningful — and a concise, plain-language, keyboard-focusable / screen-reader-reachable definition tooltip is layered on top wherever a specialist term renders.
