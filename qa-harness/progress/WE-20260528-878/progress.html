Bug summary

The shadcn Dialog overlay is set to bg-[rgb(73_61_54/0.12)] backdrop-blur-sm in src/components/ui/dialog.tsx:22. At 12% opacity warm-brown the overlay is essentially invisible: the underlying chat remains fully readable, focus does not shift to the modal, and on bright displays users may not realize a modal is open. Industry standard is a 40-60% dark scrim. Affects every dialog: Settings, Pricing, Checkout, Feedback, Auth, Vibe.
