Bug summary

WE-20260527-352 (P2): modeRouter classified "What is the meaning of the mangalsutra in Hindu weddings?" as stylist because "mangalsutra" matched the jewellery regex while the knowledge regex tied/lost on score. Model responded with styling-advice voice instead of cultural significance.

WE-20260527-360 (P3): When knowledge keywords competed with planner keywords on a single message (e.g. "what is the mangni ceremony schedule"), planner won the tie. Result: the user's clear knowledge intent ("what is …") got planner-style numbered checklist output instead of cultural explanation.
